# Message drafting

Write three messages in the prospect's market language. Step 1 is under 90 words, opens with a specific observed fact from the prospect's site or reviews (never a compliment), has one clear ask, has no link for WhatsApp, and is signed by the real sender name. Follow-ups reference step 1 and add one new reason. Follow the country channel policy and include opt-out language where required. Return strict JSON only.

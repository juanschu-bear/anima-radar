# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, React, Supabase, deployed on Vercel.

## Users

Primarily small business owners and lean sales teams who need to discover and qualify likely buyers without spending their day researching companies manually.

## Product Purpose

AnimaRadar turns a business profile into a repeatable market-radar workflow: define the offer, scan a market, review evidence-backed prospects, approve outreach, and learn from outcomes.

## Positioning

The product ranks prospects by observable buying signals and keeps the operator in control of the evidence, message, and sending step.

## Operating Context

The product is used as a focused desktop web workspace during research and outreach sessions, with compact mobile access for checking status.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Supabase provides authentication and tenant-aware persistence.
- The UI needs to expose profile, radar, prospect review, sending, and learning flows.
- Illustrative prospect data must remain clearly demo data until live records are connected.
- The operator must approve outreach manually.

## Brand Commitments

The name AnimaRadar is retained. The tone should feel intelligent, calm, exact, and quietly premium rather than loud or gimmicky.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the working Next.js shell, API routes, Supabase integration, and existing Spanish-language product copy. No approved photography, logos, or customer proof should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the signal, not a vague promise.
2. Keep human approval visible at every consequential step.
3. Make dense market information feel calm and navigable.
4. Turn every interaction into a clearer next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, touch-sized controls, and reduced-motion support are required. Motion must never be the only way to understand state.

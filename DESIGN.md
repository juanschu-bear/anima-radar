# AnimaRadar visual direction

AnimaRadar is a premium market-intelligence workspace: dark, quiet, precise, and slightly cinematic. Its visual metaphor is a private observatory where business signals resolve into an actionable constellation.

## World

- Deep ink-blue surfaces, not pure black.
- Warm champagne highlights for decisions and premium moments.
- Cyan signal light only for live/healthy system states.
- High-contrast editorial display type paired with a measured geometric UI sans.
- Hairline dividers and atmospheric light are structural, not decoration.

## Signature

The Signal Field: a restrained constellation/orbit visualization used to show that the product is actively reading a market. It should appear once as a focal moment per surface, never as a background gimmick everywhere.

## Interaction

Use motion to establish spatial continuity: a single orchestrated entrance, subtle signal drift, quick state changes, and a clear reduced-motion final state. Never animate every card.

## Do not

- Return to the previous cream/editorial prototype treatment.
- Use generic SaaS purple gradients, nested cards, emoji icons, or decorative metric rings.
- Hide important data behind hover only.

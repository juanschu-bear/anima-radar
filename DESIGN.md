# AnimaRadar visual direction

AnimaRadar is a premium market-intelligence workspace: dark, quiet, precise, and cinematic. Its visual metaphor is a private observatory where business signals resolve into an actionable constellation. The main workspace is a full-bleed stage, not a conventional card dashboard.

## World

- Deep ink-blue surfaces, not pure black.
- Warm champagne highlights for decisions and premium moments.
- Cyan signal light only for live/healthy system states.
- High-contrast editorial display type paired with a measured geometric UI sans.
- Hairline dividers and atmospheric light are structural, not decoration.
- The dashboard opens like a scene: sky, horizon, atlas, and a small daily rail create a sense of place before the operator reads metrics.
- Interfaces may borrow from cinematic title sequences and knowledge-graph tools, but every effect must explain the market signal.

## Signature

The Market Atlas: a living network of evidence links and buying signals. It is the primary visual object on the home surface and can be explored, filtered, and opened into the full radar. It should appear once as a focal moment per surface, never as a background gimmick everywhere.

## Interaction

Use motion to establish spatial continuity: a single orchestrated entrance, subtle signal drift, quick state changes, and a clear reduced-motion final state. Never animate every card.

## Do not

- Return to the previous cream/editorial prototype treatment.
- Use generic SaaS purple gradients, nested cards, emoji icons, or decorative metric rings.
- Hide important data behind hover only.
- Use a generic SaaS dashboard composition as the first viewport.
